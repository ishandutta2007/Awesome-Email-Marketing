## Top Email Marketing Platforms Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Campaign Management, Marketing Automation, Newsletter Delivery & Audience Segmentation*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Email Marketing**. These tools help businesses, creators, and marketers design campaigns, automate sequences, segment audiences, and deliver newsletters at scale.



**Examples** include Mailchimp, Kit (formerly ConvertKit), Brevo, ActiveCampaign, Campaign Monitor, MailerLite, Constant Contact, Omnisend, GetResponse, and Moosend (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom automation workflows, and transparent subscriber data — ideal for creators and businesses that want full control over their audience without per-subscriber SaaS fees or vendor lock-in.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/)**

  The most recognized email marketing platform. Provides campaign builder, audience segmentation, automation workflows, and e-commerce integrations. Free tier available.



- **[Kit (formerly ConvertKit)](https://kit.com/)**

  Email marketing platform designed for creators. Focuses on visual automation builders, subscriber tagging, and monetization tools like paid newsletters.



- **[Brevo](https://www.brevo.com/)**

  All-in-one marketing platform (formerly Sendinblue). Provides email, SMS, chat, and CRM features with transactional email capabilities.



- **[ActiveCampaign](https://www.activecampaign.com/)**

  Marketing automation platform with advanced conditional logic, machine learning, and CRM integration. Known for sophisticated automation workflows.



- **[Campaign Monitor](https://www.campaignmonitor.com/)**

  Email marketing platform with beautiful templates and drag-and-drop builder. Popular with agencies and brands focused on design quality.



- **[MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/)**

  Email marketing platform known for simplicity and affordability. Provides automation, landing pages, and website builder for small businesses.



- **[Constant Contact](https://www.constantcontact.com/)**

  Email marketing platform for small businesses and nonprofits. Offers templates, automation, and event management tools.



- **[Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com/)**

  Email and SMS marketing platform built for ecommerce. Provides automation workflows, product recommendations, and shopper segmentation.



- **[GetResponse](https://www.getresponse.com/)**

  Email marketing platform with automation, landing pages, webinars, and CRM capabilities.



- **[Moosend](https://moosend.com/)**

  Email marketing and automation platform with affordable pricing and visual campaign builder.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Listmonk](https://github.com/knadh/listmonk)**

  High-performance, self-hosted newsletter and mailing list manager with a modern dashboard. Single binary Go application with PostgreSQL backend. Features subscriber management, list segmentation, campaign templates, bounce processing, and a comprehensive REST API. Every feature in the admin UI is backed by the API. Docker deployment available. **AGPL-3.0** .



- **[Keila](https://github.com/pentacent/keila)**

  Open-source newsletter tool positioned as an alternative to Mailchimp and Sendinblue. Supports sending via your own inbox for smaller newsletters, or AWS SES, Sendgrid, Mailgun, Postmark, and SMTP for larger projects. Features block editor, Markdown support, responsive email templates, sign-up forms, and multilingual support. Docker image and Docker Compose config provided. **AGPL-3.0** .



- **[Mautic](https://github.com/mautic/mautic)**

  The most feature-complete open-source marketing automation platform. Provides email campaigns, landing pages, forms, lead scoring, CRM capabilities, and multi-channel automation. Used by organizations including DB Bahn.business and Lehner Versand AG (2 million contacts, 1 million emails daily). **GPL-3.0** .



- **[SendPortal](https://github.com/mettle/sendportal)**

  Open-source self-hosted email marketing platform. Manage newsletters at a fraction of the cost of commercial services. Laravel-based with campaign management, subscriber lists, and tracking. ~2k stars .



- **[phpList](https://github.com/phpList/phplist3)**

  Fully functional open-source email marketing manager for creating, sending, integrating, and analysing email campaigns and newsletters. One of the oldest and most mature open-source email marketing tools with 780+ stars. Supports advanced subscriber management, bounce handling, and plugins. **AGPL-3.0** .



- **[Notifuse](https://github.com/Notifuse/notifuse)**

  Open-source, self-hosted newsletter, email marketing, and transactional email platform. Visual MJML editor, Liquid templating, 7 sending providers. Features workspaces (multi-tenant), duplicate prevention for cross-list sends, conditional delivery based on segments, A/B testing, blog with custom domains, and Supabase integration for membership sites. **AGPL-3.0** .



- **[Emareach](https://github.com/ritik-prog/emareach)**

  Production-grade open-source AI email marketing platform with automation, campaigns, deliverability, analytics, and self-hosting. Focused on cold outreach and deliverability: mailbox warm-up with LLM-generated threads, spam-to-inbox recovery, SPF/DKIM/DMARC checks, multi-step campaigns, and smart lead search. FastAPI backend, Next.js frontends, MongoDB, Docker Compose, and Terraform for AWS deployment .



- **[PolyPress](https://hub.docker.com/r/tylerhats/polypress)**

  Self-hosted multi-tenant email newsletter platform and internal MTA. Features visual block designer, automated list subscription preference center, delivery queue tracking, and SQLite database. Docker deployment with persistent volumes for data, backups, and branding. Developer REST API .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Mailing List Managers**: **Mailman** (GPL-3.0, Python, the standard for discussion lists), **Sympa** (GPL-2.0, Perl, web-based installer), **Schleuder** (GPG-enabled, encrypted mailing lists) .

- **Newsletter Tools**: **Mailtrain** (self-hosted newsletter app), **RSS2Newsletter** (convert RSS/Atom to email newsletters), **tinyCampaign** (lightweight newsletter system) .

- **Email Template Builders**: **Maizzle** (build HTML emails with Tailwind CSS, 2,379 stars), **MJML** (responsive email framework) .



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Listmonk** for the core newsletter engine and API, **Mautic** for advanced automation and CRM, **Keila** for simple creator-focused campaigns, and **Maizzle** for custom responsive email templates. Add **PostgreSQL** for persistence and **Docker** for deployment.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, DNS configuration (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), and deliverability monitoring.
