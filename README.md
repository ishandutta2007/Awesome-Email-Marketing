## 📊 Sector Market Overview

> **Estimated Market Size:** The global Email Marketing Software Market was valued at approximately **$7.5 Billion** and is projected to reach **$17.9 Billion** by 2030, growing at a CAGR of **15.2%**.
> 
> **Market Dynamics:** The sector is **moderately fragmented**. While legacy giants like Intuit Mailchimp and enterprise platforms like ActiveCampaign command major market share, the industry features intense competition from specialized SaaS platforms (built for creators, e-commerce, or transactional emails) and a rapidly growing open-source self-hosted ecosystem powered by engines like Listmonk and Postal.

## 🛠️ Email Frameworks & Utilities

- **Mailing List Managers**: **Mailman** (Python, discussion lists standard), **Sympa** (Perl, enterprise mailing lists).
- **Email Delivery Infrastructure**: **Docker-Mailserver** (Full-featured mail server in Docker), **MailHog** / **Mailpit** (Developer SMTP testing tools).
- **Template Engines**: **MJML**, **Maizzle**, **Foundation for Emails** (Responsive email framework).
